How We Started

Start Right Up was founded in 2026 by two founders who know exactly what it feels like to start over in a new country. Our founders, originally from Thailand and the Philippines, first arrived in Singapore as students. Over the years, they navigated every stage of that journey themselves — from their first days in Singapore as newcomers, through the years of building a life and career here, to eventually calling it home.

That firsthand experience is what Start Right Up is built on. We've personally been through the paperwork, the waiting periods, the unfamiliar systems, and the small everyday questions that no guidebook answers. Now, we help others skip the guesswork.

We work closely with individuals, families, and corporate HR teams to provide personalized, end-to-end relocation support — from home search and moving logistics, to school search and settling-in essentials. Because we've lived the process ourselves, we understand not just what needs to get done, but how it actually feels to move to Singapore and build a life here.

What We Believe

To help others start right in Singapore — with the same guidance and support we wish we'd had when we first arrived.
