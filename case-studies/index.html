A Professional from the Philippines

Relocating to Singapore for a new role

The challenge
After accepting a job offer in Singapore, our client booked a serviced apartment for his first few weeks. He wanted to view homes in person before signing a lease. The search turned out to be harder than expected. Finding a place that suited him took longer than planned, and his serviced apartment costs kept growing.

How we helped
When he heard about Start Right Up, he got in touch. We took time to understand his needs and budget, recommended neighbourhoods near his office, and connected him with property agents for viewings. Once he had moved in, we helped him set up utilities and internet so his new home was ready to live in.

The outcome
Within a week of engaging us, he had found a home that fit his budget and gave him an easy commute to his office. He moved out of the serviced apartment and settled into his new place.

A Family from Thailand

Two parents and one child relocating to Singapore

The challenge
Moving as a family meant several things had to happen at once. The family needed an international school for their child, bank accounts for both parents, working phones and home internet, and a way to get their belongings from Thailand to Singapore. They wanted one team to help them manage it all.

How we helped
We started by getting to know the family and their move, then put together a package tailored to what they needed. Before they arrived, we held an online meeting to prepare them and go through each step together.

We shortlisted international schools with the family and helped them apply to three. We compared bank account options and shared a shortlist with the parents, and set up their accounts once they had chosen. We also had their SIM cards ready, arranged utilities and home internet, and coordinated with the mover to bring their belongings from Thailand to Singapore.

The outcome
Their child was accepted into one of the schools. Because the family got in touch early, there was enough time to prepare and submit each school application properly. The family told us they felt relieved and happy to have support. While we took care of the setup, they could focus on getting everything else ready for the move.
