Leaving Singapore, Done Right

Departure has its own deadlines, and they don't wait for you to catch up. Tax clearance with IRAS has to be cleared before your final paycheck is released. Work pass cancellation is tied to your last working day. Lease, utility, and service contracts each carry their own notice periods, and local bank accounts need to be closed or transferred properly. We coordinate all of it against one timeline, so nothing lapses early and nothing overlaps at the worst possible moment
