咨询服务

Every move begins with a conversation. Our complimentary 15–20 minute Discovery Call gives us the opportunity to understand your relocation plans, priorities and the areas where you may need support.

Whether you're moving for work, relocating with your family or planning your child's education in Singapore, we'll listen to your needs, explain how Start Right Up can support your journey and recommend the most suitable next step.

For clients who require more detailed, personalised guidance, we can arrange a dedicated Relocation Consultation or recommend the appropriate Start Right Up service based on your needs.

择校匹配

Finding the right school is about more than location or curriculum. We help families identify suitable school options based on their child's age, educational needs, preferred curriculum, budget, location and family priorities. We can assist with shortlisting schools, understanding admission requirements and arranging school visits where available, giving parents a clearer path towards making an informed choice for their child's education in Singapore.

住房搜寻

Finding a home in a new country can feel overwhelming when you're unfamiliar with the neighbourhoods, commute and local rental market. We help you narrow down suitable areas and housing options based on your budget, workplace or school location, commute and lifestyle needs. Where property transactions require a licensed professional, we can connect you with the appropriate partners to take the process forward.
