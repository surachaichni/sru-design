Getting Set Up

The first few weeks in a new country come with plenty to organise. We help make them easier — from airport pickup and getting connected with a local SIM card to guidance on Singpass, banking, utilities and home internet. We can also help you get familiar with your new neighbourhood, including nearby transport, supermarkets and everyday essentials. Along the way, we'll point you towards important local services and emergency contacts, so you know where to turn when you need help. It's practical, on-the-ground support designed to help you spend less time figuring things out and more time settling into life in Singapore.

Personal Income Tax

Moving to Singapore can also mean adjusting to a new tax system. We help you understand the practical basics of Singapore personal income tax, including tax residency, the general filing process, important dates and where to access the right government resources.

For more complex circumstances — such as overseas income, multiple sources of income or cross-border tax matters — we can help connect you with an appropriate tax professional. Our role is to make the process less unfamiliar, help you know what may require your attention and point you in the right direction, so important administrative matters don't get lost among everything else that comes with relocating.

Will Writing

Relocating to Singapore is also an opportunity to ensure that the assets you build and hold here are properly planned for. Having an appropriate will can help ensure that, should anything happen to you, your Singapore assets are managed and distributed according to your wishes to your intended beneficiaries.

We can help make this important step easier by connecting you with qualified professionals who can advise you on will and estate planning based on your individual circumstances. It's a thoughtful part of settling in — helping you protect what you've built in Singapore and provide greater clarity and security for the people who matter most.
